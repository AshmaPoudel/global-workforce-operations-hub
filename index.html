📌 Understanding EOR employment country
The country you select represents where your employment contract is officially registered — not necessarily where you are physically located. This is the core of how Employer of Record (EOR) works: your legal employer is registered in your employment country and handles local payroll, taxes, and labour law compliance on your behalf.
Digital nomad in Thailand, hired through a Thai EOR — select Thailand, enter salary in Thai Baht. Thai income tax and Social Security Fund apply.
UK employee working remotely from Thailand — select United Kingdom, enter salary in GBP. UK income tax and National Insurance apply regardless of physical location.
Nepalese national employed through a Nepali EOR, living abroad — select Nepal, enter salary in NPR. Nepali tax obligations apply regardless of country of residence.
